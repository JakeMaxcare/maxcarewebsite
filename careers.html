Our main role

Become a direct support professional.

Direct support professionals are the heart of Maxcare. You'll support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities in their homes and out in the community — helping with daily living, joining activities and outings, and being someone they can count on.

Homes run around the clock, so we have day, evening, overnight, and weekend shifts — there's a schedule that fits your life. Beyond direct support, we also hire house managers and administrative staff from time to time — our Indeed page always shows what's currently open.

01

Paid training

You don't need experience in care work to start — we provide paid training and the support to do the job well.

02

Real purpose

This is work that matters. Every shift makes a genuine difference in the lives of the people we support.

03

A team that has your back

Maxcare has been family-rooted since 1994 — 68 team members who look out for the people we serve and for each other.
