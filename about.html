About us

Three decades of care, rooted in family.

Maxcare has been part of the Albuquerque community since 1994 — a New Mexico company supporting New Mexicans with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

Our founding

It started with family.

Maxcare was founded in 1994 by a family member of a person with an intellectual and developmental disability. What began as one family's determination to build a better life has grown into eight group homes and a team of 68 people serving the Albuquerque area.

That closeness still shapes how we care. We know what it means to want the best for someone you love — because that's where we came from.

The mission of Maxcare, Inc.

To support and promote the self-selected lifestyles of New Mexicans with developmental disabilities by maximizing independence, supporting personal choice, and cultivating growth in our community.
